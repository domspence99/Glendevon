Good to Know

Booking policies

Check-in & Check-out

Check-in from 4pm on your arrival day. Check-out by 10am on your departure day.

Payment

A 25% deposit secures your booking. The balance is due 8 weeks before arrival. Payment by bank transfer.

Cancellation

Deposits are non-refundable. Cancellations within 8 weeks of arrival forfeit the full amount unless the dates are re-let. We recommend travel insurance.

Dogs

Up to 2 well-behaved dogs are welcome at no additional charge. Please keep dogs off the furniture and beds, and clean up on the coastal paths.

Smoking

Glendevon is a non-smoking property. Smoking is not permitted anywhere inside the cottage or patio.

Guests

The cottage sleeps a maximum of 4 guests. The number of guests should not exceed what was agreed at time of booking.
