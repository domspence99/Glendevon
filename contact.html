🧭 Getting Here

By car

Follow the A174 to Staithes. Park at Glen Vale long-stay car park at the top of the hill (TS13 5BH), then walk down the village lane — approximately 5 minutes.

By train

The nearest station is Saltburn-by-the-Sea (approx. 7 miles). Taxis are available from the station.

By bus

The X4 bus connects Middlesbrough, Guisborough, Loftus and Whitby, stopping at Staithes. Check current timetables at Arriva Yorkshire.
